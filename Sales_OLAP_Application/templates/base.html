⚠️ Mandatory Educational Project Disclaimer

PharmaTrack DW is an educational computer science capstone project demonstrating Data Warehousing and Data Mining (DWDM), multidimensional OLAP schemas, and public health location intelligence. It is not a medical diagnosis tool, not a guarantee of medicine stock or hospital capacity, not a payment platform, not an ambulance dispatch service, and not an organ-donor matching system. Never delay seeking professional medical treatment or calling official emergency numbers during medical crises.
