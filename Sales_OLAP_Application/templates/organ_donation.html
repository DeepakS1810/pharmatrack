What Organs Can Be Donated?

Kidneys, Liver, Heart, Lungs, Pancreas, and Small Intestine can be donated following verified brainstem death certification by accredited medical boards.

👁️

Tissue Donation

Corneas (restoring eyesight), Heart Valves, Skin, Bone, and Tendons can be donated even after circulatory cardiac death within documented recovery windows.

📜

How to Pledge Official Support?

Pledging is an expression of noble intent. Inform your immediate family members so they can uphold your wishes through official SOTTO/NOTTO nodal officers.
