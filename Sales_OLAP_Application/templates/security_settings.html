About Two-Step Verification

How TOTP verification protects your health warehouse account.

📱 What is an Authenticator App?

Authenticator apps such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, and Aegis generate a secure 6-digit code on your phone every 30 seconds based on an international open standard (RFC 6238 TOTP).

🚫 No SMS or WhatsApp Required

Unlike SMS or WhatsApp OTPs, authenticator apps work 100% offline without cellular coverage, SIM cards, or third-party telecom delivery fees.

🔑 What are Recovery Codes?

Recovery codes allow you to regain account access if you misplace your mobile device. Each code can be redeemed exactly once.

🛡️ Rate-Limited Protection

Login credentials, authenticator codes, and recovery codes are protected against brute-force guessing through progressive rate-limiting locks.
