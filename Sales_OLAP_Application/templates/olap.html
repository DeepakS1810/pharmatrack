OLAP Concept 1

🔼 Roll-up (Aggregation Upward in Dimension Hierarchy)

Roll-up summarizes detailed transaction data by climbing up a concept hierarchy. For example: aggregating from daily transactions $\rightarrow$ Month $\rightarrow$ Quarter $\rightarrow$ Year, or climbing from specific Medicines $\rightarrow$ Therapeutic Categories.

OLAP Concept 2

🔽 Drill-down (Navigating to Greater Granular Detail)

Drill-down is the reverse of roll-up. It decomposes higher-level summary aggregates into finer details, stepping from Year $\rightarrow$ Quarter $\rightarrow$ Month $\rightarrow$ Day, or from State $\rightarrow$ District $\rightarrow$ Pharmacy location.

OLAP Concept 3

🔪 Slice (Selecting One Dimension Value)

A Slice creates a 2D sub-cube by fixing exactly one dimension to a single constant value (e.g., fixing Region = 'Tamil Nadu' or Medicine = 'Paracetamol').

OLAP Concept 4

🎲 Dice (Filtering Across Multiple Dimensions Simultaneously)

A Dice operation extracts a smaller sub-cube by applying conditional filters on two or more dimensions simultaneously (e.g. Region IN ('Tamil Nadu', 'Karnataka') AND Category IN ('Tablet', 'Capsule') AND Year = '2025').

OLAP Concept 5

🔄 Pivot / Rotate (Rearranging Dimensional Orientation)

Pivot (or Rotate) reorients the multidimensional axes. It swaps row dimensions into columns or brings the Z-depth dimension into row positions, allowing analysts to view relationships from an alternate perspective.
