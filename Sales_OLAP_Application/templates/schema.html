⭐ Star Schema Architecture

Fast Analytical Query Performance

In a Star Schema, central fact tables connect directly to denormalized dimension tables via single-hop foreign keys. Descriptive attributes (such as category names or state codes) are embedded inside dimension records to eliminate complex multi-table joins and maximize OLAP aggregation speed.

  • Grains: 1 record per sales order; 1 per weather observation; 1 per pharmacy stock snapshot; 1 per hospital service report.
  • Structure: Single-hop FK → PK joins (Fact → Dimension only).
  • Advantage: Simpler analytical SQL, faster aggregations, easier indexing.
  • Trade-off: Data redundancy in dimension tables (repeated category/state text).

❄️ Snowflake Schema Architecture

Normalized Hierarchies (Third Normal Form)

In a Snowflake Schema, dimension tables are normalized into related sub-dimension tables. Dimensional hierarchies branch outward like ice crystals, enforcing referential integrity and eliminating data redundancy.

  • Normalized Hierarchies: dim_category & dim_dosage_form → dim_product; dim_state → dim_district → dim_city → dim_pharmacy & dim_hospital.
  • Structure: Multi-hop FK relationships forming normalized trees.
  • Advantage: Zero storage redundancy, centralized master data management.
  • Trade-off: Requires multi-table JOIN operations for analytical reporting.
